Hi, I'm Margaret Muthoni

I like knowing where the money went. After it is approved and spent, did it do what it was meant to do?

I started in audit. For two years at PwC Kenya I tested expenditure, procurement and supporting evidence on donor-funded and public sector programmes, followed up exceptions, and reported control weaknesses to management. As a senior associate I led audit teams of 3 to 5 people.

That work left me curious about what sits behind the numbers: which spending is off track, which records don't match, and which patterns are easy to miss. Power BI, SQL and advanced Excel let me answer those questions faster and show the answers clearly.

I'm building my career around one idea: testing where money goes and showing what it delivers.
