About

I spent two years in assurance at PwC Kenya, auditing donor-funded and public sector programmes. I tested expenditure and procurement against evidence, followed up exceptions, and reported control weaknesses to management. As a senior associate I led audit teams of 3 to 5 people.

That work left me with one habit: I want to know what happened to the money after it was approved, and whether it did what it was meant to do. Data tools let me answer that faster and show it more clearly.

I pair that background with Power BI, SQL and advanced Excel, and I'm building case studies that turn programme and financial data into dashboards people can act on. I'm based in Nairobi and looking for financial analyst, programme analytics and MEL roles with development organisations and NGOs.
